About Us

AKR Devi Memorial PU College

AKR Devi Memorial PU College (RK0112) is a co-educational higher secondary institution serving rural students in Sriramnagar, Koppal. Established in 2005-2006, it is managed by an unaided administration and offers classes 11 to 12.

The college is focused on academic excellence, student support, and a strong learning environment for higher secondary education in Karnataka.
