AKR Devi Memorial PU College

AKR Devi Memorial PU College (Institution Code: RK0112) is a premier co-educational higher secondary institution in Sriramnagar, Koppal district, Karnataka. Founded with the mission to bridge the educational divide, the college has established a benchmark in academic discipline and state board results.
